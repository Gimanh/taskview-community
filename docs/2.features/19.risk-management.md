# Risk Management & Heatmap Matrix

Identify, quantify, and mitigate delivery risks proactively before they impact budget, schedule, or product quality.

## 5×5 Probability-Impact Heatmap Matrix

TaskView visualizes the project risk exposure using an interactive 5×5 matrix:

| Probability / Impact | 1 - Very Low | 2 - Low | 3 - Medium | 4 - High | 5 - Critical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **5 - Almost Certain** | 5 (Medium) | 10 (High) | 15 (Critical) | 20 (Critical) | 25 (Critical) |
| **4 - Likely** | 4 (Low) | 8 (Medium) | 12 (High) | 16 (Critical) | 20 (Critical) |
| **3 - Possible** | 3 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 9 (Medium) | 12 (High) | 15 (Critical) |
| **2 - Unlikely** | 2 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 8 (Medium) | 10 (High) |
| **1 - Rare** | 1 (Low) | 2 (Low) | 3 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 5 (Medium) |

### Severity Classifications
- **Critical (15–25)**: Immediate executive escalation required.
- **High (10–14)**: Active mitigation plan and weekly review.
- **Medium (5–9)**: Standard monitoring by project leads.
- **Low (1–4)**: Documented and accepted risk tolerance.

## Risk Register & Workflow

Each risk item includes:
- **Category**: Schedule, Cost, Technical, Resource, or External.
- **Severity Score**: Automatically calculated as $\text{Probability} \times \text{Impact}$.
- **Mitigation Strategy**: Preventative measures designed to reduce probability or impact.
- **Contingency Plan**: Secondary actions triggered if the risk materializes.
- **Ownership & Status**: Assigned risk owner and status lifecycle (Identified $\to$ Evaluating $\to$ Mitigating $\to$ Closed / Accepted).
