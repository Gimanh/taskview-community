SCIM lets your identity provider (IdP) manage who is in your TaskView organization. The IdP creates people, changes their details and turns their access off; TaskView follows. Groups from the IdP can be linked to projects, so people who join a group are added to the project with a role you choose.

SCIM works on top of an [SSO configuration](/docs/features/sso): each SSO config has its own SCIM token and its own set of provisioned users and groups.

## Turning SCIM on

1. Open your organization's settings → **SSO** tab. You need the **admin** or **owner** role.
2. Make sure the SSO email domain is verified (see [Domain verification](/docs/features/sso#domain-verification)). SCIM requests are refused with `403` until it is.
3. In the SSO config card, switch on **SCIM Provisioning**.
4. Click **Generate SCIM Token** and copy the token. It is shown only once; TaskView stores only its hash. **Regenerate Token** replaces it, and the old token stops working.
5. In your IdP, set the SCIM base URL to the **SCIM Endpoint URL** shown in the card (`<API URL>/scim/v2`) and the token as a Bearer token.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-network" color="warning"}
Behind a reverse proxy, set [`API_PUBLIC_URL`](/docs/configuration/environment-variables#application) so the SCIM Endpoint URL points to an address your IdP can reach.
::

## Turning SCIM off

Switch off **SCIM Provisioning** in the SSO config card. Then:

- **The token is deleted.** Requests from the IdP are refused with `401`.
- **Nothing is removed.** People stay in the organization and its projects. SCIM users, the remembered access of deactivated users, groups and their project links stay in the database. The **Groups** list is hidden while SCIM is off.
- **Group links stop working**, since the IdP no longer sends changes.
- **SSO sign-in adds people again.** As without SCIM, anyone from the SSO domain who signs in is added to the organization with the default role (**member**).

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="warning"}
This includes people the IdP deactivated over SCIM. They get back into the organization with the default role and no projects: their remembered role and projects are restored only by a reactivation over SCIM. If they must stay out, block them at the IdP before you switch SCIM off.
::

### Turning it back on

1. Switch **SCIM Provisioning** on and click **Generate SCIM Token**: the old token is gone.
2. Put the new token into your IdP.

The kept SCIM users, groups and links apply again, and changes the IdP sends from then on are handled as usual. Changes made in the IdP while SCIM was off reach TaskView only if the IdP sends them again.

A deactivated person who got back in while SCIM was off stays in the organization, but their next SSO sign-in is refused, since their SCIM record is still deactivated. For the IdP they are already deactivated, so it may never send anything about them: remove them from the organization by hand.

## People

| What the IdP does | What happens in TaskView |
|---|---|
| Creates an active user | The person is added to the organization with the **member** role. Someone who is already a member keeps their role. |
| Creates an inactive user | Nobody is added to the organization. |
| Changes name or email | The SCIM record is updated. If the person already has a TaskView account, a new address is applied to it at their next SSO sign-in; otherwise their invitation to the organization moves to the new address. |
| Deactivates a user (`active: false`) | The person is removed from the organization and from all of its projects, their SSO link and sessions are deleted. TaskView remembers their organization role and projects with roles. |
| Reactivates a user | The remembered organization role and projects come back, plus the projects of groups linked or joined while the person was deactivated (see [Groups and projects](#groups-and-projects)). |
| Deletes a user | Same as deactivation, and the SCIM record is deleted. |

Rules that always apply:

- **Email.** TaskView takes the address from `emails` (the primary one, otherwise the `work` one, otherwise the first). If there is none, `userName` is used when it is an email address. Without an address the request is refused with `400`.
- **Domain.** The address must belong to the SSO config's email domain, otherwise the request is refused with `400`.
- **Owner.** The organization owner cannot be deactivated or deleted over SCIM (`400`).
- **Existing members.** When the IdP looks a person up by `userName` or email and they are already a member of the organization (added by hand or before SCIM was turned on), TaskView links them to the IdP instead of creating a duplicate. Only addresses in the SSO domain are linked.
- **Members added by hand** who the IdP never sent are not touched by SCIM.

## Signing in with SCIM on

While SCIM is on, the IdP decides who may enter the organization. When someone signs in through this SSO config:

- an **active** SCIM user or an existing member of the organization signs in as usual;
- a **deactivated** SCIM user is refused with *"Your access to the organization is turned off"*;
- anyone else is refused with *"You have not been added to the organization yet"*: SSO no longer adds new people on their first sign-in.

With SCIM off, SSO works as described in [User provisioning](/docs/features/sso#user-provisioning): new people are added on their first sign-in.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-info" color="info"}
If you remove a person from the organization by hand while the IdP still has them as active, they come back with the SSO config's default role (**member**) at their next SSO sign-in. To keep someone out, deactivate them in the IdP.
::

### When SSO and SCIM disagree

In the IdP, access to the application over SSO and provisioning over SCIM are often set up separately, so they can get out of step:

- **Deactivated over SCIM, but the IdP still lets them sign in.** TaskView refuses the sign-in: with the same address the person sees *"Your access to the organization is turned off"*, with an address SCIM has not seen *"You have not been added to the organization yet"*. Their sessions were already closed when SCIM deactivated them.
- **Blocked from signing in at the IdP, but still active over SCIM.** The IdP stops new sign-ins, but TaskView has not been told anything: the person stays in the organization and its projects, and sessions that are already open keep working until they expire.

::callout{icon="i-lucide-alert-triangle" color="warning"}
To take someone's access away completely, make sure the IdP deactivates or deletes them **over SCIM** (sends `active: false` or `DELETE`). Only that removes them from the organization and its projects and closes their sessions right away.
::

## Groups and projects

Groups sent by the IdP appear in the SSO config card under **SCIM Provisioning → Groups** (visible while SCIM is on), with the number of members. A group can be linked to one or more projects, each with a **default role**.

SCIM **only adds** people to projects. It never removes anyone and never changes the roles of someone who is already in a project:

| Event | Result |
|---|---|
| A group is linked to a project | Everyone currently in the group is added to the project with the default role. |
| A person joins a linked group | They are added to the linked projects with the default role. |
| The person is already in the project | Nothing changes: their roles stay as they are. |
| A person leaves the group | Nothing changes: they stay in the project. |
| The link is removed | Nothing changes for people already in the project; new group members are no longer added. |
| Someone removes the person from the project by hand | They stay out. Syncs of the same group do not bring them back. |

A person who was removed from a project by hand is added again only by a new event: they leave the group and join it again, they join another group linked to the same project, or the link is removed and created again.

People are added only if they are an active SCIM user **and** a current member of the organization. Archived projects are not filled, and the project owner is skipped.

### Linking a group

1. In the **Groups** list, click **Add project** next to the group.
2. Choose a project and a default role. The role must exist in that project; create it in the project's settings first if needed.
3. Click **Link**.

Linking the same project again with another role changes the default role for people who join later. People already in the project keep their roles.

Who can link: organization **owners** and **admins**, the same people who manage SSO. They can link any active project of the organization, even one they are not a member of. In this list they see only project and role names; opening a project still requires being a member of it.

### What else changes a link

- The IdP deletes the group: its links are deleted, people stay in the projects.
- The project or the role is deleted: the link is deleted.
- The project is archived: the link stays, but nobody is added while the project is archived.

## Supported SCIM operations

Base path: `/scim/v2`. Every request needs `Authorization: Bearer <SCIM token>`.

| Method | Path | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/ServiceProviderConfig`, `/ResourceTypes`, `/Schemas` | Discovery |
| GET | `/Users` | Filters: `userName eq` (case-insensitive), `externalId eq`, `emails eq`, `emails.value eq`, `emails[type eq "work"].value eq`. Pagination with `startIndex` and `count` (default 100, max 200) |
| GET, PUT, PATCH, DELETE | `/Users/{id}` | Deactivated users stay readable with `active: false` |
| POST | `/Users` | `409` if the `userName`, email or `externalId` is already used in this SSO config |
| GET | `/Groups` | Filters: `displayName eq`, `externalId eq`. Supports `excludedAttributes=members` |
| GET, PUT, PATCH, DELETE | `/Groups/{id}` | Members must be users of the same SSO config, otherwise `400` |
| POST | `/Groups` | `409` if the `externalId` is already used in this SSO config |

Group names do not have to be unique: two groups may share a `displayName`. Only `externalId` identifies a group.

## Provider notes

**Pocket ID**

- Restrict the OIDC client used for SCIM to user groups. Otherwise Pocket ID also sends its built-in API user, which has no email, and the sync fails with `400`.
- During a sync Pocket ID deletes SCIM users it did not create itself, such as ones connected by another provider. If it has a user with the same email, that user takes their place; otherwise the person is removed from the organization. Members added by hand and never sent over SCIM are not affected.
- When a user is taken out of a group on the user's page, Pocket ID v2.18.0 does not send the change. Change membership on the group's page instead. Leaving a group does not remove anyone from a project anyway, but TaskView keeps showing the person in the group.

## API endpoints for group links

Require authentication and the organization **admin** or **owner** role.

| Method | URL | Description |
|---|---|---|
| GET | `/module/sso/admin/configs/{configId}/scim-groups` | Groups with their links, and the projects with roles that can be linked |
| PUT | `/module/sso/admin/configs/{configId}/scim-groups/{groupId}/projects/{goalId}` | Link a group to a project. Body: `{ "roleId": number }` |
| DELETE | `/module/sso/admin/configs/{configId}/scim-groups/{groupId}/projects/{goalId}` | Remove the link |

## Database tables

| Table | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `tv_auth.scim_users` | Users provisioned over SCIM, per SSO config, with the remembered access of deactivated users |
| `tv_auth.scim_groups` | Groups sent by the IdP |
| `tv_auth.scim_group_members` | Who is in which group, and since when |
| `tv_auth.scim_group_projects` | Links between groups and projects with the default role |
