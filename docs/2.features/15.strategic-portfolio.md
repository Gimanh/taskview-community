# Strategic Portfolio & KPI Linkage

TaskView provides full traceability from high-level corporate strategy down to individual project execution.

## Strategic Hierarchy

```
Strategic Objectives (Executive Vision & Horizon Goals)
       │
       └── Strategic Initiatives (Targeted Programs & Funding)
                 │
                 └── Initiative Projects (Execution Workstreams)
                           │
                           └── Strategic KPIs (Quantifiable Metrics & RAG Health)
```

## Multi-Tier Reporting Dashboards

1. **Executive Tier Dashboard**:
   - Organization-wide health overview.
   - Aggregate strategic progress percentage.
   - Total budget allocation and spend velocity.
   - Portfolio RAG breakdown (On Track, At Risk, Off Track).

2. **Portfolio & Initiative Tier**:
   - Initiative-level budget utilization and milestone schedules.
   - Cross-project dependencies and strategic contribution weights.

3. **Project & KPI Drilldown**:
   - Baseline, Current Value, and Target Value per KPI.
   - Direct links into project Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and sprint backlogs.
