# Annual Planning Exercise Automation

Streamline annual and quarterly planning exercises by managing intake proposals, objective scoring, and project conversion within a unified workflow.

## Planning Cycles

- **Fiscal Year Scoping**: Define cycles (e.g., FY2026 Strategy Intake) with submission deadlines and budget envelopes.
- **Envelope Control**: Track requested funding against organizational budget ceilings in real time.

## Intake Proposals & Business Cases

Department leads and project sponsors submit proposals containing:
- Problem statement and proposed solution.
- Targeted strategic objective.
- Capital (CapEx) and operational (OpEx) budget requests.
- Estimated delivery timelines.

## Multi-Criteria Scoring Matrix

Proposals are scored across three dimensions (0–100 scale):
1. **Strategic Alignment (40%)**: Degree of fit with core corporate objectives.
2. **ROI & Financial Viability (35%)**: Payback period, cost reduction, or revenue expansion.
3. **Execution Feasibility & Risk (25%)**: Resource availability and complexity.

$$\text{Total Score} = (S \times 0.40) + (R \times 0.35) + (F \times 0.25)$$

## 1-Click Project Promotion

Approved proposals can be converted directly into active delivery projects with a single click. The system automatically initializes standard stagegates, assigns leadership, and links the project to the strategic initiative.
