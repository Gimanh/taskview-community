# Automated Progress Reporting & Reminders

Ensure continuous stakeholder visibility and proactive risk mitigation with structured progress reporting and automated notification reminders.

## RAG Status Health Tracking

Project leaders submit periodic status reports evaluating four core dimensions using standard Red-Amber-Green (RAG) indicators:

- **Overall Health** — Composite delivery prognosis.
- **Schedule Health** — Milestone timeliness and critical path slippage.
- **Budget Health** — Expenditure velocity and cost variance.
- **Scope Health** — Requirement stability and change request backlog.

Each report also captures:
- Key achievements during the reporting period.
- Critical blockers and escalation requests.
- Next scheduled milestones and upcoming deliverables.

## Cadence Configuration & Automated Reminders

- **Reporting Frequencies**: Configure weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly reporting cycles per project.
- **Automated Alerts**: The background scheduler alerts project owners via in-app notifications and email before reports are due.
- **Audit History**: All historical progress reports remain accessible for governance and retrospective reviews.
