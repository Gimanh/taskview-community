# Stagegates & Approval Cycles

Stagegates enforce enterprise governance across the project delivery lifecycle. Each project progresses through structured phases, requiring designated stakeholder review and sign-off before entering the next phase.

## Standard Delivery Phases

TaskView comes pre-configured with industry-standard project delivery stages:

1. **Initiation & Concept** — Business case evaluation, feasibility study, and project charter sign-off.
2. **Planning & Design** — Scope baseline, WBS decomposition, resource commitments, and architecture review.
3. **Execution** — Core deliverables implementation, sprint cycles, and milestone execution.
4. **Monitoring & Control** — Quality assurance, variance analysis, change control board approvals, and user acceptance testing.
5. **Closure & Handover** — Operational handover, post-implementation review, contract closeouts, and lessons learned.

## Exit Criteria Checklist

Before a stagegate can be submitted for approval:
- Required exit tasks must be completed and marked as finished.
- Deliverables must meet acceptance thresholds.
- Any critical blocking risks must have approved mitigation plans.

## Approval Review Workflow

When all exit criteria are satisfied, the project manager submits the gate for review:
- Designated gate approvers receive notification alerts.
- Approvers review deliverables and can provide formal review comments.
- Approvers mark the decision as **Approved** or **Rejected**.
- Every decision is captured in an immutable audit trail with timestamp and reviewer identity.
