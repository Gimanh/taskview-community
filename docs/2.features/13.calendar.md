The calendar shows a project's tasks on the days they are scheduled. It answers questions a list can't answer quickly: what's due this week, which days are overloaded, and what's coming up next.

## Opening the calendar

Select a project in the sidebar, then click **Calendar**. On mobile, open the menu and tap **Calendar**.

## Which tasks appear

A task appears on the calendar when it has a **start date**, an **end date**, or both:

- **Start and end date** - the task spans every day in between
- **Only one date** - the task appears on that day
- **A time** - the time is shown on the task card, in your local time zone

Tasks without dates don't appear. To place a task on the calendar, open it and set a start or end date.

## Views

- **Month** - the whole month at a glance. Days with more tasks than fit show a "+N more" link.
- **Week** - one column per day, with every task listed as a card. Best for planning the week. This is the default view on desktop. Saturday and Sunday shrink to a narrow column when nothing is scheduled on them.
- **List** - the week's tasks grouped by day. This is the default view on mobile.

Use **Today** and the arrows to move between periods. The selected view and period are kept in the page address, so a reload or a shared link opens the same place.

Click a day number to jump to that week in the list view.

## Reading a task card

- **Color** - the task's priority: red for high, amber for medium, green for low
- **Avatars** - who the task is assigned to
- **Subtask count** - shown when the task has subtasks
- **Strikethrough** - the task is complete

Click a card to open the task details. Changes you make there show up on the calendar right away.

## Permissions

The calendar follows the project's existing permissions: you only see tasks you're allowed to see, and priority colors and assignees only appear if your role can view them.
